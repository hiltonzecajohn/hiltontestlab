Our Story

Built From the Ground Up In Mozambique

Haps Soluções LDA was established in 2006 and has grown into one of Mozambique's most respected engineering and fleet solutions companies. Starting as a specialist workshop in Matola, we have expanded over 16+ years into a full-service enterprise with branches from Maputo to Nacala and Pemba.

We specialize in the mechanical and automotive industry, serving mining, construction, transport and logistics, cargo handling, manufacturing and power generation sectors. Our team of over 135 skilled employees delivers consistent, high-quality solutions across Mozambique, backed by regular internal training programs.

As official Allison Transmission service dealer, WABCO Mozambique distributor, Powerstar trucks dealer, and Shantui equipment dealer — we combine local expertise with world-class brand partnerships to deliver the best solutions for your fleet.
